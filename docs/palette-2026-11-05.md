# Palette ratio — measured state of the closing plates

Companion to the review on 2026-11-05 that asked for the accent-plate + ink-plate
composition (already passing in PR #3) on the tree that is live.

This branch carries that composition applied to the live tree (`main` @ d2862d0):
every page closes with an accent plate (black type on `#6CACE4`, 8.66:1, 4px black
rule, black action with accent label) and an ink-plate footer (white field, black
type 21:1), plus the mark, contact links and privacy line. It does **not** yet sit
inside the bands of `PALETTE-SPEC-2026-11-05.md`, and the reason is structural
rather than a missing value:

| page | doc height @1440 | black% | white% | accent% | band verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| home | 7170 | 80.28 | 11.24 | 8.48 | black 0.28 over |
| learn | 2277 | 48.43 | 26.30 | 25.27 | over on accent + ink |
| merch | 2157 | 45.22 | 28.09 | 26.69 | over on accent + ink |
| giveaways | 2407 | 50.98 | 25.11 | 23.91 | over on accent + ink |
| affiliates | 1910 | 38.57 | 31.39 | 30.04 | over on accent + ink |

Phone (390) is the same shape, 1–4 points worse on ink.

The closing plates are a fixed pixel height; the bands are a percentage of the
delivered surface. On a 7170 px home page a 600 px accent plate is 8.5% and the
page passes. On a 1910 px affiliates page the same plate is 30%, and the ink
footer is 593 px = 26% — the plate *content* alone (label, headline, paragraph,
action; contact links, footer columns, meta line) is ~460 px before any padding,
so no padding value can bring a 1910 px page under a 14% accent ceiling.

Two ways out, and the choice is Tyler's:

1. **Tune per page** — keep the bands as a hard rule on every page and give the
   short pages a compact closing band (headline + action only; drop the plate
   label and the body paragraph), measured per page. Composition changes on
   learn / merch / giveaways / affiliates.
2. **Scope the bands to the fold** — apply the ratio to what a visitor sees
   without scrolling (1440x900 / 390x844) rather than to the whole scroll
   height. That is what the reference example in the spec measures, and the
   composition above is built to satisfy it.
