Consulting

AI systems
for businesses.

The same local-first stack, built for companies. Set up hands-on with the client, then tuned for two to four weeks on feedback from the people who use it every day. Ongoing updates run under a service contract, and going fully on-premises is a planned later phase, not a rebuild.

A

Territory dashboards

A fast, colour-coded snapshot a rep can read in twenty minutes before a meeting: sales, weights, quarter on quarter, year on year, refreshed automatically from the systems the business already runs.

B

A CRM of your own

A full custom CRM built on dotBANG's own white-label platform, shaped to one company's pipeline instead of rented by the seat. Follow-up reminders and customer history included from day one.

C

AI throughout

Plain-language summaries of what changed, alerts when a number moves, and follow-ups drafted before they are due. It runs on the client's own AI accounts and keys, never on ours.
