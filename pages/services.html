Engagements

Three ways to work with dotBANG.

Every engagement starts with the data and ends with something your team uses on a Tuesday. Scope is written before work starts, payment follows delivery, and the source is yours.

01 · The data proof

One week, fixed fee. Three of your real data drops loaded and reconciled against your own reporting, the territory and account mapping written down, and an honest assessment of whether the numbers are readable at all. No dashboard is built in this week.

This is the step that stops an eight-week project from starting on a guess. If the drop is unusable, you find out for a week's cost instead of a project's cost.

What you get

  • A reconciled load of three drops, with the differences against your reports named
  • The territory and account mapping, written, for you to confirm once
  • A short written assessment: what is readable, what is not, what it will take

02 · The first version

Eight weeks, four phases, paid per phase. Ingestion, per-user territory scoping, five screens, the generated brief, a pilot with two or three real users in week five, hardening in week six, rollout and handover in weeks seven and eight.

Nothing is invoiced for a phase that has not been delivered, and acceptance is written into the scope as tests rather than adjectives: totals within 0.1% of your own report for a sampled month, a new user completing the walkthrough in twenty minutes, the refresh running unattended for five consecutive business days.

What is deliberately not in version one

  • Order entry, quoting or invoicing — your ERP stays the system of record; the dashboard only reads
  • Commission calculation — it shows the sales and weight commission is calculated from
  • Forecasting and pipeline scoring — two guesses stacked on a new dashboard
  • A full CRM — reminders attached to accounts are in; contacts, deals and email sync are not
  • A native mobile app — a responsive site covers phones without an app store

03 · Model operations

Monthly, cancellable. For teams running models on hardware they own: serving health checks that also send a real completion (a green /health on its own is a false green), model swaps with a rollback path, configuration drift monitoring wired to a human, and a runbook for the day it breaks.

This is not advisory. dotBANG's own cluster serves production agent workloads every day, and the same checks that keep it alive are what you get.

How an engagement runs

Where it runsThree options: your infrastructure (data never leaves your network, we hand over a container and a deployment script), dotBANG-hosted for a pilot (fastest to stand up, encrypted, full export at any time), or embedded in your existing stack (lowest adoption friction, most constraint). Same application in all three.
Who owns the sourceYou do. The repository is handed over at the end, or transferred into your organisation at the start if you prefer.
What is excludedChanges to your ERP, historical clean-up beyond about 36 months, and any phase-five work that turns into a new project.
What we need from youThree recent data drops, the territory and account list, the user list, one named contact for reporting questions, and one pilot user who is willing to be sceptical.
PaymentPer phase, on delivery. No deposit on the whole programme.

Scope and pricing are agreed per engagement. The first conversation is free and the data proof is the first invoice.
